Questions, answered

Everything you need to know before you start.

What is sellersPro?

sellersPro is a simple web dashboard for small online sellers. You can record orders, keep your products and stock organised, save customer details, print receipts and read your sales reports in one place.

Is sellersPro free?

Yes. You can start on the Free plan, which includes 10 orders every month. No credit card is needed to sign up.

Who is sellersPro for?

It is made for small sellers and home businesses who take orders through a shop, Facebook, WhatsApp or phone calls and want to stop tracking everything in notebooks and spreadsheets.

Do I need to install anything?

No. sellersPro runs in your web browser, so you can use it on a phone, tablet or computer.

Is my business data private?

Yes. Your products, customers and orders are stored under your own account and are not visible to other sellers.
